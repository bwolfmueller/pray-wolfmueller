_To the chief Musician, A Psalm of David._

<sup>1</sup> The LORD hear thee in the day of trouble;
  The name of the God of Jacob defend thee;
<sup>2</sup> Send thee help from the sanctuary,
  And strengthen thee out of Zion;
<sup>3</sup> Remember all thy offerings,
  And accept thy burnt sacrifice; _Selah_.
<sup>4</sup> Grant thee according to thine own heart,
  And fulfil all thy counsel.
<sup>5</sup> We will rejoice in thy salvation,
  And in the name of our God we will set up _our_ banners:
  The LORD fulfil all thy petitions.
<sup>6</sup> Now know I that the LORD saveth his anointed;
  He will hear him from his holy heaven
  With the saving strength of his right hand.
<sup>7</sup> Some _trust_ in chariots, and some in horses:
  But we will remember the name of the LORD our God.
<sup>8</sup> They are brought down and fallen:
  But we are risen, and stand upright.
<sup>9</sup> Save, LORD:
  Let the king hear us when we call.
