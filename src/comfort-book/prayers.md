## The Kyrie

Lord, have mercy upon us.
Christ, have mercy upon us.
Lord, have mercy upon us. Amen.

## For the Sick

O Great Physician of body and soul, our lives are in Your hands. Hear our prayer in our trouble and need. Visit us with Your kindness. Help us in our need. Give us health of body and mind. And give us the wisdom and faith to receive this trouble as Your loving discipline and and give from Your paternal care, thought Jesus Christ, Your Son, our Lord, who lives and reigns with You and the Holy Spirit, One God, now and forever. Amen.

## For Patience

O Holy Spirit, You promise to bear fruit in the lives of Your people. You dwell in me by the gift of baptism. Bear in me the fruit of patience and long-suffering. Teach us to wait for You, and give us the confidence that you will not cast us off forever, but that You will hear and answer our prayers. Amen.

## For Those Near Death

Heavenly Father, give to Your servant a blessed death in the faith. Forgive their sins. Strengthen them for this, their last hour. Sustain them in their agony. Give them the confidence of Your promises that never fail. Send Your holy angels to carry the soul of Your servant to Your face, and the perfect joys of heaven. Protect their body to the day of the resurrection of all flesh. Give to us who live the confidence to die in Your name, and depart in peace, knowing that Your Son Jesus is the resurrection and the life. Amen.

## For Those Who Mourn

O Lord Jesus Christ, You are raised from the dead and seated at the Father’s right hand. Send to us Your Holy Spirit, the Comforter, to those who mourn. Give them the confidence of Your promises, the promise of the forgiveness of sin, the resurrection of the body, and the life everlasting. Give us faith in Your Word and promises, that we would come to the joys of those who have gone through death to Your eternal life, for You live and reign with the Father and the Holy Spirit, One God, now and forever. Amen.

## For Those Who Travel

Heavenly Father, Your Son Jesus has promise to be with us always, even to the end of the age. Be with us as we travel. Send Your angels to protect and keep us from all dangers. Bless our going out and our coming in, for this time forth, even forever more. Amen.

## For Wisdom

O Lord, You have promised that if anyone lacks wisdom, they need only ask, and You will provide. We need wisdom. Give is to us. Teach us to fear You, to love You, to trust in You in all our ways. Make our path straight. Keep our feet from falling. Teach us according to Your Word. Amen.

## For Humility

Dear Father in heaven, You cast down the proud and lift up the humble. Humble us with Your law. Lift us up with Your Gospel. Keep us in Your kindness in life and in death, that knowing Your love we might begin to love You and our neighbor. Amen.

## For the Holy Spirit

Dear Jesus, You have promised that the Father will send the Holy Spirit to those who ask. Send us Your Holy Spirit. Cleanse our conscience. Forgive our sins. Grant Your Word to abide in us and bear in us the fruit of repentance and faith. Show us our sin. Strengthen our faith in Your promises. Keep us steadfast in Your Word. Amen.

## For Grace

O Lord, whose mercy is new every morning, and whose faithfulness is great, give us grace this day to rejoice in Your kindness and know Your love. Smile upon us, now and always. Amen.

## For an Answer to Prayer

Lord Jesus Christ, You have promised to hear and answer our prayers. Give heed to the petitions we bring to You now. Hear us. Answer us. Deliver us. Keep us, not for our sake, but for the sake of Your blood and righteousness. Amen.
